Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?
Entirely on your device. Daily Arc List has no account system and no servers — your tasks stay local, and follow your device's iCloud backup settings if you have those enabled in iOS Settings.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. Daily Arc List never requires an internet connection.
How do I mark a task complete?
Tap the circle next to a task, or swipe it and choose Complete.
How do I delete a task?
Swipe left on a task and tap Delete.
Can I set a due date or priority?
Yes — when adding or editing a task, toggle "Set a due date" and choose a Low, Medium, or High priority.
How do I find a specific task?
Use the search bar at the bottom of the task list, or switch between the All, Active, and Completed filters.
I found a bug. What should I include in my report?
Please describe what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps too. Send it to vipinpahwadev@gmail.com.
