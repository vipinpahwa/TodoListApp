Privacy Policy for Daily Arc List

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Daily Arc List is a to-do list app for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it. In short: nothing leaves your device.

Data collection

Daily Arc List does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on any server. The app has no backend, no analytics, and no advertising SDKs. It does not know who you are and does not ask.

Where your data lives

Every task you create — its title, notes, due date, priority, and completion status — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data stays on your device and in your personal iCloud backups (if you have device backup enabled in iOS Settings). We have no access to it, ever.

Tracking

Daily Arc List does not track you or your activity, and does not share any information with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. There are no third-party SDKs in this app.

Network access

Daily Arc List does not require or use an internet connection. It works entirely offline.

Children's privacy

Since Daily Arc List collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data collection — this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
